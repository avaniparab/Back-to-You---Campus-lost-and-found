About Back to You

"Back to You" is a student-driven campus utility created to replace informal WhatsApp message chains and chaotic physical notice boards with a unified, transparent Lost & Found network.

Every semester, hundreds of essential student items—textbooks, graphing calculators, keys, earphones, and student identity cards—are misplaced across campus facilities. Back to You establishes structured reporting, authentic verification, and verified handover points to ensure personal belongings return safely to their owners.
